Design a complete mobile app for "World of Rice" — a premium Indian rice e-commerce brand. The app sells heritage and regional rice varieties online. Use a clean, warm, food-first design aesthetic.

COLOR PALETTE (strictly follow these):
- Primary Green: #1A5C38 (buttons, headers, active states, navbar highlights)
- Accent Gold/Amber: #D4A017 (highlights, price labels, badge accents, "UPDATED" text)
- Dark Brown: #2C1F0E (footer backgrounds, dark sections)
- CTA Button: #E74C1F (primary action buttons like "Buy Now", "Checkout")
- Secondary CTA: #1A5C38 (outlined or filled green for "Add to Cart", "Continue Shopping")
- Background: #F5F0E8 (cream/off-white page background)
- Navy: #1C3557 (cart table headers, data tables)
- White: #FFFFFF (cards, input fields)
- Body text: #1A1A1A, Muted text: #6B6B6B

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Logo/Brand: Serif or display font, gold color
- Headings: Bold sans-serif, dark brown or dark green
- Body: Regular sans-serif, 14–16sp
- Price labels: Semi-bold, gold or green accent

DESIGN STYLE: Warm, premium, earthy. Food photography-forward. Rounded cards. Generous whitespace. Traditional yet modern.
